  Corresponding source:
    - mpv:        https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv (commit named in VERSIONS.txt)
    - build recipe and pinned dependency sources:
                  https://github.com/shinchiro/mpv-winbuild-cmake
  The NetsuCast release that shipped this installer also carries, or links to, the matching
  sources (see docs/licensing.md in the NetsuCast repository).

d3dcompiler_43.dll
  Microsoft Direct3D shader compiler, redistributed unmodified as part of the mpv build above,
  under the Microsoft DirectX redistributable terms.

yt-dlp (yt-dlp.exe)
  https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp - The Unlicense (public domain). Text: licenses\yt-dlp-Unlicense.txt.

Deno (deno.exe)
  https://github.com/denoland/deno - MIT License. Text: licenses\deno-MIT.txt.
